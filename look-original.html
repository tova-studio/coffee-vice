About us

Welcome to the Home of Coffee Vice

If coffee is your Vice, then we are here to feed it!

Coffee Vice is an independently owned, Christchurch-based family business that has grown to include owner operators. With our mobile coffee van we can cater for all your needs, whether it's a regular workplace visit or a boss's shout, a school fair, a private function, a sports club or recreational event, or a corporate event.

Our coffee beans are Certified Organic 100% Arabica Fair Trade beans, imported through Trade Aid Organization of New Zealand and freshly roasted by Ris'tretto Espresso. We're proud to serve their coffee every day.

To get Coffee Vice to come to you or your event, just contact Christel on 027 588 3744, Paul on 022 401 9911, or email info@coffeevice.co.nz.

Quarry Cafe by Coffee Vice

New Coffee and Food Trailer for Coffee Vice

Find us at Halswell Quarry

Quarry Cafe by Coffee Vice is based at Halswell Quarry, 7 days a week from 8am to 4pm. Stop by for our fine Ris'tretto coffee along with muffins, toasties, burgers, cold drinks, shakes and ice creams.

It's an easy stop on a walk around the quarry, and a relaxed place to sit at the picnic tables with a coffee and something to eat.

Questions we're often asked

Yes. Coffee Vice is a Christchurch based mobile coffee service specialising in workplace visits and work site visits, whether it's a regular visit or a one-off boss's shout.

What kinds of events do you cater for?

Where is the Quarry Cafe and when is it open?

Quarry Cafe by Coffee Vice is at Halswell Quarry, 185 Kennedys Bush Rd, Christchurch, and it's open 7 days a week from 8am to 4pm.
